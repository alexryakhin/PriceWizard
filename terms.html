2. What the app does

Price Wizard connects to App Store Connect with an API key you provide and lets you plan and apply subscription and in-app purchase prices, introductory offers and product localizations for your own apps. It works on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

3. Your account and API key

  • You need an App Store Connect API key with sufficient access (App Manager or Admin). You’re responsible for having the right to manage the apps you connect.
  • Keep your key secure. You can revoke it at any time in App Store Connect.

4. Changes made to App Store Connect

Applying prices, introductory offers or localizations changes live data in your App Store Connect account, which can affect what customers pay. The app shows a review before each apply, but you are responsible for checking the results. Prices are subject to Apple’s rules, price points, exchange rates and taxes, which can change. Pricing suggestions (for example, by purchasing power) are estimates, not financial advice.

5. Free applies and the lifetime purchase

  • The app includes 5 free applies. An apply is each time you send prices, an introductory offer or localization changes to App Store Connect. Retrying failed territories does not count again.
  • After that, a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase unlocks unlimited applies for the Apple ID that bought it, on its devices. The price is shown in the App Store for your country.
  • Payment is processed by Apple. Purchases are verified with RevenueCat. Use Restore Purchases to unlock on another device.
  • Demo mode is free and never writes to App Store Connect.
