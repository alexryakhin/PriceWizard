Requirements

  • macOS 15 or later
  • App Store Connect API key with Key ID, Issuer ID, and .p8 file (Manager or Admin)

Get your key in App Store Connect → Users and Access → Integrations → App Store Connect API. Download the .p8 once - Apple won’t show it again.

We don’t collect or receive your data. Your API key stays in your Mac’s Keychain. The app talks only to Apple’s API, RevenueCat (for in-app purchase entitlements), and a public exchange-rate service for USD conversion. See our Privacy Policy for details.
