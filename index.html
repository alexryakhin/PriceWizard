How it works

From first launch to updated prices across 175+ countries in four steps.

  • 1

    Connect App Store Connect

    Use your App Store Connect API key (Key ID, Issuer ID, and .p8 file). PriceWizard stores it only in your device’s Keychain and talks directly to Apple.

  • 2

    Choose base price and strategy

    Select your US base price and pick a pricing strategy: Apple Equalization, Netflix-style PPP, or Spotify-style PPP. You can override any territory.

  • 3

    Review every territory

    Preview the resulting price per territory, in local currency and USD, before you apply anything. Adjust outliers until it feels right.

  • 4

    Apply safely in one go

    Apply changes across all territories from one screen. PriceWizard skips territories that already match your target price and won’t touch dates that already have a scheduled change.

Requirements

  • iOS 17 or later
  • macOS 15 or later
  • App Store Connect API key with Key ID, Issuer ID, and .p8 file (Manager or Admin)

Get your key in App Store Connect → Users and Access → Integrations → App Store Connect API. Download the .p8 once - Apple won’t show it again.
